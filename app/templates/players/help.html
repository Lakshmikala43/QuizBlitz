Battle Rules & Scoring Guide

Everything you need to know to climb the ranks in Quiz Royale.

1. Authoritative Scoring Model

Scores are calculated authoritatively on the server. The faster you respond with a correct answer, the more bonus points you earn.

Easy Questions 100 Points
Medium Questions 200 Points
Hard Questions 300 Points

Speed Bonus Formula

Answering within the first 50% of the countdown awards up to 50 additional speed bonus points:
Speed Bonus = max(0, int(50 * (1 - (response_time_ms / (time_limit_ms * 0.75)))))

Streak Multipliers

Consecutive correct answers increase your streak. Each streak step adds +10% bonus up to a 5-streak cap (1.5x multiplier). Any wrong answer or timeout immediately resets your streak to 0.

2. Level Progression (XP Curve)

Your level reflects your cumulative battlefield mastery. Experience Points (XP) are awarded upon completing matches and unlocking achievements:

Cumulative XP Required = ceil(100 * (Level - 1) ^ 1.5)
  • Level 1: 0 XP
  • Level 2: 100 XP
  • Level 5: 800 XP
  • Level 10: 2,700 XP
  • Level 25: 11,758 XP

3. Fair Play & Security Safeguards

  • No Client-Side Answers: Correct answers are never sent to your browser ahead of time.
  • Server Timestamp Verification: The server tracks question start and elapsed milliseconds. Client clock manipulation is ineffective.
  • Idempotent Submissions: Rapid clicking or duplicate packet replay will not award multiple points.
  • Daily Challenge Limit: Strictly one ranked entry per player per calendar day.
